One funny line, at most 200 characters, any topic — a phone's autocorrect tells the truth. `main.ts` (Node 22+, no dependencies) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

> My phone autocorrected "I'm on my way" to "I'm on my weigh," which is the most honest thing it has ever said about my life.

123 characters, one sentence. An everyday truth (autocorrect always strikes at the worst moment) with the surprise at the end: the typo turns out to be the most honest thing the phone ever said.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Writes `out/entry.txt` (UTF-8, one line, 123 characters).
